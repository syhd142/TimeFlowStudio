# Trademarks and project identity

The MPL-2.0 license covers source code; it does not grant permission to use the
TimeFlow Studio name, logo, icons, or other project branding to identify a
modified or independently distributed product.

Unmodified official builds may accurately state that they are TimeFlow Studio.
Forks and modified distributions should use a different product name and visual
identity, and must not imply endorsement by the TimeFlow Studio maintainers.

Accurate statements such as “based on TimeFlow Studio” are permitted when they
do not create confusion about who publishes or supports the modified product.
